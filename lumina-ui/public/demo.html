How It Works

Three-layer scraping engine with AI fallback

1

Python Scrapers

Playwright for JS-heavy sites (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook). Fast HTTP for GCC classifieds (Expatriates, OLX, OpenSooq).

2

Apify Actors

10 Apify cloud actors for Google Maps, Reviews, Search, YouTube, Twitter/X, E-Commerce, and Website Content.

3

AI Fallback

When scrapers return empty, AI generates realistic leads with correct area codes, street names, and business details.

4

Enrich & Score

Auto-crawl websites for emails/phones. AI scoring from 0-100. Hot (70+), warm (30-69), cold (<30) classification.
